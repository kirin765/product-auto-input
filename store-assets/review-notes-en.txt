Product Auto Input 0.2.0 is a local form-prefill utility for Korean marketplace seller centers. It does not save, submit, publish or purchase products.

Free plan: 5 form fills per local calendar day. Optional paid plan: ₩4,900/month via Paddle, no fill limit. Product drafts and page content never leave the browser.

Offline popup checks (no account needed): Install, open the toolbar popup, choose a marketplace, click 예시 (sample), edit the product JSON, close/reopen to verify local draft persistence. Download CSV 템플릿 / XLSX 템플릿, fill one product, then use CSV/XLSX 가져오기 to import. Invalid values show correction fields. JSON export is available with 내보내기. The account block displays 무료 0/5회 and 계정 연결; no account is required for the free checks.

Account and payment flow (optional): Click 계정 연결. A product-site tab opens at product-auto-input.onnurimun.com. Sign in with any email via magic link, approve the extension connection, return to the popup and click 승인 확인. The popup then shows the connected email. The 구독 시작 button opens a Paddle-hosted checkout; the pricing page is public. The extension host permission for product-auto-input.onnurimun.com is used only for account linking, entitlement refresh, and opening checkout/portal; product data is not sent. Reviewers do not need to complete a payment to review the extension.

Form checks require the reviewer's own authorized seller account on a supported marketplace. No extension-service login, payment, API key or developer credentials are required or collected for the free checks. Open a product-registration page on sell.smartstore.naver.com, wing.coupang.com, www.esmplus.com / item.esmplus.com, or soffice.11st.co.kr. Use a harmless product with title, price and stock. Choose the correct marketplace, click 폼 스캔 (scan), then 폼 채우기 (prefill). Review matched fields and do not save/submit. The extension never clicks registration buttons. For multi-step forms, run separately at each step. Existing marketplace sessions are used; CAPTCHA and verification are handled by users.

Coverage is partial: selected fields checked on SmartStore, Coupang step 1, ESM Plus and 11st. Coupang price/options and Zigzag/Ably fields remain unverified. Those limitations are disclosed in the listing.

Data stays in extension-local storage. The product backend stores only account email, login sessions, revocable extension tokens, and Paddle customer/subscription/refund mappings; it never receives product drafts or marketplace page content. No analytics, advertising, remote logging or remote code is used. User-provided image URLs may be fetched with credentials omitted. Images, options and category controls may require manual work.
